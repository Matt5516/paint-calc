How much paint for an accent wall?

Usually one gallon; a quart only covers a small wall.

A 12 × 8 ft accent wall is 96 sq ft and needs 0.55 gallon for two coats: buy 1 gallon (about $45). A 16 × 9 ft wall (144 sq ft) needs 0.82 gallon.

Wall area = width × height, no doors or windows, 350 sq ft per gallon (PaintCare’s Buy Right guide’s rough-estimate figure). Deep, saturated colors need more coats: PaintCare says changing between very dark and very light, and reds, oranges and purples, may take three or four, so look at the three-coat column.

For a wall with a window or door, subtract 15 sq ft per window or 20 sq ft per door, or enter them in the wall paint calculator. Primer under a dark color: one coat, 250 sq ft per gallon (primer calculator).

FAQ

How much paint do I need for an accent wall?

A 12 × 8 ft wall needs 0.55 gallon for two coats, so a quart is not quite enough and one gallon is plenty.

Is a quart enough for an accent wall?

A quart covers about 88 sq ft per coat, so only a small wall or a single coat. Most accent walls need a gallon.

How many coats for a dark accent wall?

Two to three; very dark or bold colors can take more (PaintCare).
