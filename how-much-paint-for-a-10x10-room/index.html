How much paint for a 10 × 10 room?

Gallons, cans and cost for a 10 × 10 ft room, a small bedroom, nursery or home office.

A 10 × 10 ft room with 8 ft walls, one door and two windows has 270 sq ft to paint and needs 1.54 gallons for two coats (0.77 for one coat). Buy 2 gallons, about $90. The ceiling adds 0.57 gallons.

Assumptions: a 10 × 10 ft rectangular room, 1 door (20 sq ft) and 2 windows (15 sq ft each) subtracted, paint covering 350 sq ft per gallon (the figure PaintCare’s Buy Right guide suggests when you do not know your paint’s rate), and typical 2026 prices ($45 a gallon, $20 a quart, $200 a 5-gallon bucket). The perimeter is 40 ft.

Primer

If the walls are new drywall, stained or going from dark to light, one coat of primer at 250 sq ft per gallon is 1.08 gallons: 1 gallon + 1 quart, about $45 at typical primer prices. See primer by room size.

FAQ

How many gallons of paint for a 10x10 room?

With 8 ft walls, one door and two windows, 0.77 gallon for one coat and 1.54 gallons for two coats. Buy 2 gallons.

How much paint for a 10x10 room with 9 ft ceilings?

310 sq ft of wall, so 1.77 gallons for two coats: 2 gallons, about $90.

How much does it cost to paint a 10x10 room?

About $90 in paint for the walls at 8 ft with two coats ($45 a gallon, $20 a quart, $200 a 5-gallon bucket). The ceiling adds about $45.

How much paint for a 10x10 ceiling?

100 sq ft, so 0.57 gallons for two coats (1 gallon).
