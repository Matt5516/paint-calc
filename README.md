# CoatMath

Free paint calculator that tells you exactly which cans to buy: gallons, quarts or 5-gallon buckets, with cost. Rooms, ceilings, exterior, primer. No signup.

**Live site: [https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/)** (free, no signup, runs in your browser)

## Tools
- [Paint calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/paint-calculator/): Rooms, doors, windows, coats: how much paint, and exactly which cans to buy. Ceiling, primer and trim too.
- [Wall paint calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/wall-paint-calculator/): Add walls one at a time, subtract openings, and size an accent wall to the quart.
- [Ceiling paint calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/ceiling-paint-calculator/): Flat or vaulted ceilings by length and width or square feet. Gallons and the cans to buy.
- [Exterior house paint calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/exterior-paint-calculator/): House size, stories, gables, doors and windows, siding type: gallons and 5-gallon buckets.
- [Square footage calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/square-footage-calculator/): Rectangles, L-shapes, circles and triangles, added or subtracted. Sq ft, sq yd and m².
- [Primer calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/primer-calculator/): Area, surface and coats: gallons of primer and the cans to buy.
- [Paint cost estimator](https://matt5516.github.io/paint-calc/paint-cost-calculator/): DIY paint and supplies cost, optionally against a pro quote.

## Data sources
Paint needs follow standard geometry (wall area = perimeter × height, minus doors and windows; gallons = area × coats ÷ coverage). Coverage ranges of 300 to 400 sq ft per gallon, 350 as a rough default, and notes on textures, sheen and primer come from PaintCare's Buy Right guide (paintcare.org/buy-right). Default prices are typical 2026 US figures that you can edit.
