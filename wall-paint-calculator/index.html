The short answer

A 12 × 8 ft accent wall is 96 sq ft. Two coats at 350 sq ft per gallon need 0.55 gallon, so buy 1 gallon, about $45. At the default prices a gallon is cheaper than the three quarts you would need ($60).

Accent wall tips

  • Measure the width of the wall and the floor-to-ceiling height. Subtract any window or door on that wall.
  • A dark accent over a light wall may need a primer or an extra coat: see the primer calculator.
  • One gallon is usually the answer for a single wall, with plenty left for touch-ups.

FAQ

How much paint do I need for an accent wall?

A typical 12 × 8 ft wall is 96 sq ft. Two coats at 350 sq ft per gallon is about 0.55 gallon, so buy a gallon (or two quarts if your store sells a color you want in quarts).

How do I calculate paint for one wall?

Multiply width by height, subtract windows and doors, multiply by coats and divide by coverage per gallon.

Can I add several walls?

Yes. Use Add another wall. Each wall has its own doors and windows.
